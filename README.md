# backstreet-girls
According to the National Library of Medicine (2017), the Stroop Test is a neuropsychological test to determine cognitive interference when processing stimulus with more than one stimulus attribute. In the context of the Stroop test, the word of a color and the ink of the word could either be congruent or incongruent. Congruent means that the word of a specific color and the ink are a match, while incongruent means the word and ink are not a match. For instance, a congruent example could be the word saying "RED" and the ink of the word being red. An incongruent example could be the word saying "BLUE" while the ink of the word is green. The significance of the Stroop Test is to determine the so-called "Stroop effect," a measurement of mental delay between the brain's automatic habit of reading the word verus paying attention to ink color.
